Service guide

Learn about the service, the cases that may benefit, the steps to submit a request, and the documents that help speed up the review.

What is this service?

This service transports patients when their condition requires an urgent or specialized transfer, or when transport by other means is unsuitable.

It coordinates moving the patient from their current location to a facility capable of providing the appropriate care.

Cases eligible for the service

Eligibility varies with the medical assessment and the approved procedures. Submitting a request does not mean final approval.

Steps to submit and track your request

Make sure the data is accurate, the attachments are clear and the required information is complete. Missing or unclear documents may delay the review.
